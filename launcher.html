FAQ ❓

Why does Windows Defender / SmartScreen warn on first launch?

Because this is an independent open-source Python/community build without a paid corporate EV certificate, Windows SmartScreen may show a warning on new releases. All mod downloads are SHA-256 verified, and the launcher source is open for inspection on GitHub.

Can I play standard servers without mods?

Yes! Switch the toggle on Home to vanilla and press Play — MelonLoader is safely bypassed without removing your installed mods.

Can I import my own .dll mods?

Yes, via the Mods tab (+ Import .dll). Note that custom/unverified client DLLs may not be permitted in competitive MakeIt matches.
